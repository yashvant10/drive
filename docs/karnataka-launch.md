# Karnataka launch questions

Resolve these questions with a Karnataka transport lawyer, the relevant
regional transport office, a tax adviser, and the insurer before taking
commercial bookings. The answers depend on the contract, actual dispatch
model, vehicle use, geography, and payment flows.

- Ask the transport office whether paid driver dispatch for customer-owned
  private cars requires an operator or aggregator licence under the Motor
  Vehicles Act and applicable Karnataka rules. Confirm required licence
  categories, verification and records. Consult the [Karnataka Transport Department](https://transport.karnataka.gov.in/) and the [Ministry of Road Transport and Highways](https://morth.nic.in/).
- Confirm that the owner's motor policy covers a hired driver and clarify
  accident handling, damage evidence, liability, personal injury and the
  platform's coverage. Consult [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in/).
- Assess consent, notices, permitted uses and retention of driving licences,
  location and payment information under applicable data-protection law.
  Confirm currently effective rules and breach procedures with counsel.
  Consult [MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/).
- Review GST registration, classification, invoicing, marketplace
  obligations, commissions and withholding with a tax adviser. Consult [CBIC GST](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/).
- Review the booking, cancellation, refund, pricing and grievance terms
  against consumer-protection requirements. Consult the [Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/).
